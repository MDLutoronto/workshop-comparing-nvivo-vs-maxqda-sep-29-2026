# Workshop Recording: Comparing NVivo vs MAXQDA (Sept. 29, 2026)

This page provides a video recording of the Map and Data Library's workshop on comparing NVivo vs MAXQDA.

**Workshop Description:**

**What:** As an NVivo user, come see what the similarities and differences are between [NVivo](https://lumivero.com/products/nvivo/) and [MAXQDA](https://www.maxqda.com/), two qualitative data management and analysis tools.

**Why:** Learning how they compare will allow you to make an informed choice about which tool would be best for your research.

**Software:** This is not a hands-on workshop, but if you want to explore these tools on your own, faculty, staff, and students at the University of Toronto can get a free license key for both [NVivo](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/X7T8tmMpjP) and [MAXQDA](https://mdlutoronto.github.io/MAXQDA-installation-license/). You can also use both tools in the [Robarts Library](https://library.utoronto.ca/library/robarts) 4th floor [computer lab](https://library.utoronto.ca/space/robarts-library/robarts-library-electronic-classroom) and the Map & Data Library (MDL) [data lab](https://library.utoronto.ca/space/mdl/map-data-librarys-data-lab) on the 5th floor.

**Who:** This workshop is primarily designed for NVivo users who are new to MAXQDA. 

**How:** This is a 2-hour **online** session (but a shorter recording time due to activity breaks), consisting of a lecture and demonstrations, highlighting key similarities and differences between the two tools. *Note that this is NOT a hands-on workshop. If you would like hands-on training on using either tool, visit our other [NVivo](https://mdlutoronto.github.io/tutorials-search/?tool=NVivo&series=MDL+Workshops) and [MAXQDA](https://mdlutoronto.github.io/tutorials-search/?tool=MAXQDA&series=MDL+Workshops) workshops.*
